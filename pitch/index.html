Why it compounds

A gap in Grade 6 becomes a wall in Grade 9.

Knowledge isn't a list, it's a graph. Miss equivalent fractions in Grade 6, and adding fractions, ratios and proportions, and every algebraic expression built on them wobble in Grade 9.

Traditional tests show the symptom, a failed sum like 3/4 + 1/6. They never show the missing prerequisite underneath it.
