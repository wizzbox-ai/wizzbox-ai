# pitch_nov2: what changed from /pitch

Built 1 November 2026 for the Meridian School, Hyderabad meeting. The original deck in `/pitch` is untouched. Open `index.html` in a browser (arrow keys to move, F for full screen, Cmd/Ctrl+P to save as PDF).

## Positioning
- The lead message is now **one-on-one teaching, not testing**. Cover: "A personal tutor for every student in the class." Tagline: "Others test. WizzBox teaches."
- Lessons are now pillar 01 and diagnosis is pillar 02 (diagnosis is how the tutor knows what to teach).
- The old cover line, "Teach every student like they're your only student", is now the closing slide.

## New slides
- **3 · Why now:** the CBSE board paper is now about 50% competency-based, there are two board exams a year for Class 10, and the next one is in February.
- **5 · Others test, WizzBox teaches:** question banks and test apps compared with WizzBox (replaces "Introducing WizzBox").
- **10 · From a live classroom:** the HCF evidence, with 0 of 12 and 1 of 16 correct (from the Grades 4–10 brochure).
- **11 · Already teaching in a CBSE classroom:** 90 students, 2,435 questions, 43 lessons, 174 different questions, and the two approved teacher quotes.
- **14 · What each person gets:** management, principal, teachers, parents, students (replaces the old "What success looks like").
- **17 · The offer:** Option A (₹750 pilot with a refund guarantee) and Option B (₹250 plus ₹500 per student who improves), the annual prices and the usage clause.

## Updated slides
- **Loop:** Diagnose, Teach, Re-check, Advance (brochure wording). "A loop, not a report."
- **Curriculum:** Maths, Physics and Chemistry; CBSE, ICSE, IB and Cambridge; Grades 8–10 ready now, 4–7 being built (was Maths and Science, Grades 6–12).
- **Getting started:** 8 weeks, Class 10, finished before preboards. The school sets and marks its own baseline and end tests (was a 4-week pilot).
- **How it fits:** "No extra period. It's the homework they already do", with 20–30 minutes a day and one 40-minute training.
- **Problem slide:** 30 students instead of 40 (Meridian caps classes at about 30).
- `SCHOOL_NAME` is set to "Meridian School" in the script at the bottom.
